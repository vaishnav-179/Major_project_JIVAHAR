Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions.

What types of food can I donate?

We accept freshly cooked food (prepared within the last 4 hours) and non-perishable raw ingredients. We do not accept expired or spoiled items.

How long does it take for a pickup?

Once an NGO accepts your donation, a volunteer is assigned immediately. Pickups typically happen within 1-2 hours of acceptance.

Can I cancel a donation?

Yes, you can cancel a donation before a volunteer is assigned to pick it up. Go to 'My Donations' and click 'Cancel'.
